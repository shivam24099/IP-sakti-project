# Ayurvedic Formulations: General Overview

An Ayurvedic formulation typically combines one or more medicinal plant ingredients according to principles described in classical texts, such as ratios, processing methods, and intended use. Manufacturing and sale of Ayurvedic formulations in India is regulated by AYUSH-related drug licensing authorities, which govern requirements such as manufacturing standards, labelling, and safety.

A new formulation that combines known ingredients in a genuinely novel ratio, processing method, or for a new therapeutic application not previously documented may have a stronger basis for patent protection than a claim over a single well-known ingredient's traditional use alone.
