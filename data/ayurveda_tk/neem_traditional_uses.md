# Traditional Uses of Neem (Azadirachta indica)

Neem has a long history of traditional use in Indian Ayurvedic practice. Its leaves, bark, and oil have traditionally been used for skin conditions, as an antimicrobial agent, and in oral-care preparations. Neem oil has also traditionally been used in pest-control and agricultural contexts.

Because these traditional uses are widely documented in classical and modern Ayurvedic literature, a patent claim covering only the "general antimicrobial use of neem" would likely face novelty objections, since this is considered existing traditional knowledge rather than a new invention.
