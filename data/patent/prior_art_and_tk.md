# Prior Art and Traditional Knowledge

Prior art includes any information that has been publicly disclosed before the filing date of a patent application, in any form, anywhere in the world. This includes documented traditional knowledge, such as entries in the Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL), which is used by patent examiners in India and abroad to check whether a claimed invention is already known.

When a patent application relates to a formulation using a plant or biological resource that has documented traditional use, examiners will typically check TKDL-type sources to confirm whether the specific formulation, or only a general use of the resource, was already known.
