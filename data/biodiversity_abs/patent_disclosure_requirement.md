# Disclosure of Biological Source in Patent Applications

Indian patent law requires an applicant to disclose the source and geographical origin of any biological material used in an invention, where such material is used in the specification. Failure to disclose this, or providing false information about the source, can be a ground for opposition or revocation of the patent.

This means a patent application for an Ayurvedic formulation using an Indian biological resource, such as a specific medicinal plant, should typically be accompanied by evidence of compliance with Biological Diversity Act requirements, including NBA approval where applicable.
