# Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Basics

Under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, accessing India's biological resources for research or commercial use -- including for filing a patent application based on such a resource -- generally requires prior approval from the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) or the relevant State Biodiversity Board.

This requirement applies even when the underlying traditional knowledge about the resource, such as the traditional use of a specific plant, is already publicly known, because the ABS requirement concerns access to the biological resource itself, not only the novelty of the invention.
